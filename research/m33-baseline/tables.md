## Per-row additive cost model

The cut *d* is the smallest time removed from every Zeebra attempt that brings the equal-worker ratio's 95% upper bound below 1 (bisected on the same bootstrap). "share" is *d* as a fraction of Zeebra's median process wall; a stage share above 100% means that stage alone cannot close the gap. "median gap" is the cut that only brings the median ratio to 1, the floor under *d*.
